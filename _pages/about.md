I build computer vision and deep learning systems for the physical world — object
detection, multi-object tracking, sensor fusion, camera geometry, and 3D reconstruction —
and ship them through production MLOps pipelines.

I'm a **Senior Machine Learning Engineer at [Derq](https://www.derq.com/)** in Dubai, where I
lead research on label-free detector training, vulnerable-road-user tracking, and automated
camera calibration for intersection safety systems. I'm also a **Graduate Research Assistant at
the Georgia Institute of Technology**, working with the Human-Augment Analytics Group under
[Dr. Arthur Porto](https://agporto.github.io/markdown-cv/) on neural photogrammetry of museum
specimens, while completing an M.S. in Computer Science.

My work tends to live where research meets deployment constraints: methods that have to hold up
on an unseen site, on the hardware actually available, without a two-week annotation cycle for
every new location.

- **Selected work** — five case studies across domain adaptation, 3D reconstruction, and
  multi-sensor tracking are on the [projects](/projects/) page, including a
  [Stage&nbsp;1B winner](/projects/dot-challenge/) of the US DOT Intersection Safety Challenge.
- **Background** — the full history is on my [CV](/cv/).
- **Core areas** — Deep Learning · Computer Vision · Geometric Computer Vision · Statistical
  Learning. Mostly Python and C++, with PyTorch, and MLOps on AWS SageMaker, Kedro, Docker, and
  Kubernetes.
